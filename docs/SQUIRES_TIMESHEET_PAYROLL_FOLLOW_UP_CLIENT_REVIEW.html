02

What changed

One card for each item that is now in the Squires App.

Generic timesheet PDF

  • The old Working in Yard column is now Job number / Yard.
  • If a job number is entered, it appears in that column.
  • If there is no job number and the day was yard work, the cell shows Yard.
  • Remarks now hold comments, leave text and subsistence only.
  • The Plant timesheet PDF is unchanged.
Civils / Transport form On-screen tick still says Working in Yard

Overnight Double Time

  • If finish is earlier than start (a shift that crosses midnight), Civils, Plant and Others now pay the whole shift as Double Time, even if Night Shift was not ticked.
  • The manual Night Shift tick still works for same-day nights.
  • Transport / Lorries still has no night premium. Those hours stay on the usual weekday / Saturday / Sunday bands.
  • Bank holidays are still Double Time for every rule set.
Differs from the signed “employee ticks Night Shift” wording

Payroll Received

  • Only Accounts and Admin can mark Payroll Received.
  • Team managers, including Neil, can only mark Manager Approved after Accounts have acted.
  • Transport sheets stay in Pending until Accounts mark Payroll Received.
  • The designed order is unchanged: submit → Payroll Received → Manager Approved.
Applies to every team

Individual payroll overrides

  • Accounts / Admin can now save an employee override without a full rules activation.
  • An employee can stay on their operational team (for example Civils) and still be paid on Plant, Transport, Civils or Others.
  • Paul Jankiewicz and Dave Johnson are ready to be set to Plant payroll, but have not been written on the live accounts yet. That wait is for your go-ahead and the Sunday the change should start.
Pending your go-ahead
03

What we checked / confirmed

Plain-language results from the August 2026 follow-up work.

  • The signed July 2026 matrix is still in force: Lorries, Civils, Plant (39 hours via 8 hours Monday–Thursday and 7 hours Friday), and Others; a 30-minute unpaid break after more than 6 hours; bank holiday Double Time; leave reported as days; Basic, Overtime and Double Time kept as separate raw hours; Plant travel and IPR at 0.2 per worked day, capped at 1.0.
  • A Saturday 16:00 overnight 12-hour shift now calculates as Double Time for Civils, Plant and Others (the Adrian-style case), including the 30-minute break. It is no longer treated as Saturday Overtime when the shift crosses midnight.
  • Transport approval order is Payroll Received first (Accounts or Admin only), then Manager Approved. A team manager can no longer mark Payroll Received.
  • The Plant override path for named employees exists in Admin Settings. Paul Jankiewicz and Dave Johnson have not been set on the live system. The next Sunday from this document is 30 August 2026, if you confirm those names and that start week.
  • The generic timesheet PDF now shows job numbers in Job number / Yard, and remarks no longer start with “Job number”.
  • Already-approved and already-processed weeks were not recalculated. Historical snapshots stay as stored, including any week that already showed the overnight problem.
Historical weeks stay locked. If a past week was already marked Payroll Received or Manager Approved, the stored breakdown remains. We will only reopen specific weeks if you ask us to.

How to use the new admin control

Assign an employee to a payroll rule without moving their operational team.

1

Find the employee

Search by name. Keep them on their current operational team if that is still correct.

2

Choose the payroll rule

Select Plant, Transport, Civils or Others, or “Use team rule” to remove an override.

3

Choose the Sunday

Pick the week-ending Sunday the change should start. The same Sunday cannot later be rewritten for that person.

4

Save employee overrides

Use the separate Save employee overrides action. Do not use Activate new payroll versions for a two-person exception.
