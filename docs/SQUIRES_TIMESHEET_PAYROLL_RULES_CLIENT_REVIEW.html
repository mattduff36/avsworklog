02

Rules understood from the email

This is the calculation matrix proposed for implementation.

Lorries

  • Monday–Friday: all payable hours at Basic.
  • Saturday: first 4 payable hours at Overtime; remaining hours at Double Time.
  • Sunday: all payable hours at Double Time.
  • Deduct a 30-minute unpaid break when elapsed work is more than 6 hours.
  • No separate night-shift premium applies.

Civils

  • Monday–Friday: all payable hours at Basic.
  • Saturday–Sunday daytime: all payable hours at Overtime.
  • Night shift: the whole shift at Double Time.
  • Deduct a 30-minute unpaid break when elapsed work is more than 6 hours.

Plant

  • Monday–Thursday: first 8 payable hours at Basic; remaining hours at Overtime.
  • Friday: first 7 payable hours at Basic; remaining hours at Overtime.
  • Saturday: first 4 payable hours at Overtime; remaining hours at Double Time.
  • Sunday or night shift: the whole shift at Double Time.
  • Deduct a 30-minute unpaid break when elapsed work is more than 6 hours.
  • Operator travel is totalled separately for the week and excluded from pay-band hours.
  • IPR is 0.2 units per worked day, capped at 5 days / 1.0 unit per week.

Others

  • Monday–Thursday: first 8 payable hours at Basic; remaining hours at Overtime.
  • Friday: first 7 payable hours at Basic; remaining hours at Overtime.
  • Saturday: first 4 payable hours at Overtime; remaining hours at Double Time.
  • Sunday: all payable hours at Double Time.
  • Deduct a 30-minute unpaid break when elapsed work is more than 6 hours.
  • Night shift: the whole shift at Double Time.

Rules applying across the system

Area Proposed behaviour
Break calculation When elapsed work exceeds 6 hours, deduct 0.5 hours before splitting the remaining payable hours into Basic, Overtime or Double Time.
Night shifts The employee or authorised editor selects “Night Shift” for the day. For Civils, Plant and Others, the whole shift is Double Time. Lorries has no separate night-shift premium.
Time precision Round entered start and finish times to the nearest 15-minute interval. Display and export final totals to two decimal places.
Subsistence Retain the manual “Stayed Away” marker for all employees. Export the number of qualifying days and the relevant day names; payroll applies the payment externally.
Paid leave Report paid and unpaid leave as separate day counts only, including half days where applicable. Do not convert leave into hours; paid leave is settled using average earnings outside the hours calculation.
Bank holidays All hours worked on a bank holiday are Double Time for every payroll rule set.
Pay-treatment totals Keep Basic, Overtime (1.5× treatment) and Double Time hours separate. Do not multiply hours when calculating worked-hour totals; for example, 39 Basic + 12 Overtime + 4 Double Time remains 55 worked hours, shown in three separate buckets.
Historical records Once approved, the calculation is stored as an immutable snapshot. Later rule edits cannot silently change a previously approved or processed payroll result.

04

Client clarifications incorporated

The answers received on 27 July 2026 are now part of the specification.

Plant example corrected A 07:30–18:00 Plant shift is 10.5 elapsed hours. After the 30-minute unpaid break, it produces 8 Basic hours and 2 Overtime hours.
Leave remains in days Paid and unpaid leave are reported as separate day counts. Leave is not converted to hours because paid leave uses an average-earnings payment.
Bank holidays All hours worked on a bank holiday are Double Time for Lorries, Civils, Plant and Others.
Overtime treatment Overtime remains the 1.5× pay treatment, but the system records raw Overtime hours separately and never multiplies them into worked-hour totals.
Others night shifts A night shift under the Others rule set is paid wholly at Double Time.
Quarter-hour precision Entered start and finish times are rounded to the nearest 15-minute interval.
Worked-hours example: 39 Basic hours + 12 Overtime hours + 4 Double Time hours is reported as three separate treatments totalling 55 worked hours—not 65 multiplied hours.
05

Proposed administration controls

A controlled rules area inside the existing Squires App.

A new panel will be added under Admin Settings → Timesheets. Full Admin and Super Admin users will be able to view, add, update, assign and retire payroll rule sets.

Structured editor Admins choose controlled fields such as break threshold, daily Basic limit and weekend bands. Arbitrary formulas or executable code cannot be entered.
Effective dates Every activated version has a start week. Edits create a new version rather than silently rewriting the past.
Safe deletion Unused drafts can be deleted. A rule already used for payroll is archived and remains available for audit.
Test calculator Admins can enter example times and preview the result before activating a rule.
Activation safeguard: a rule cannot become active while a required value is missing or contradictory. Payroll export will explain the missing configuration rather than calculate an assumed result.
06

How calculations will appear

The same result will be visible throughout the workflow.

1

Employee entry and confirmation

The employee enters start/finish times, which are rounded to 15-minute intervals, plus travel, subsistence and any Night Shift selection, then sees the calculated weekly breakdown before submission.

2

Manager approval

The approver sees separate Basic, Overtime and Double Time hours, paid/unpaid leave days, travel, IPR and subsistence alongside the original entries.

3

Approved snapshot

Approval locks the rule version and calculated result used for that timesheet, protecting the payroll audit trail.

4

PDF and payroll reports

The approved detail, PDF and Excel payroll export all use the same locked calculation, including weekly Plant travel, IPR and subsistence.

07

Quality and payroll safeguards

Testing will focus on boundary cases and historical stability.

  • Test every weekday and weekend rule for Lorries, Civils, Plant and Others.
  • Test the break boundary at exactly 6 hours and immediately above 6 hours.
  • Test Saturday’s first 4 hours and the transition into Double Time.
  • Test nearest-15-minute rounding, overnight shifts and manual Night Shift selection, including Others.
  • Test full-day and half-day paid/unpaid leave counts with no conversion into hours.
  • Test bank-holiday Double Time for every rule set.
  • Test that Basic, Overtime and Double Time remain separate raw-hour buckets with no multiplier applied to total worked hours.
  • Test Plant weekly travel and the IPR five-day / 1.0-unit cap.
  • Test team assignment, employee overrides and Civils fallback behaviour.
  • Test that later rule edits cannot alter approved or processed historical payroll.
  • Test Admin permissions, draft validation, safe deletion and rule archiving.
  • Cross-check employee preview, manager view, PDF and Excel export totals.
